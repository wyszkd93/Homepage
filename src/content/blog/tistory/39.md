**1\. 조회 쿼리**

```sql
select i.instance_name
       ,sum(s.value/(86400*(sysdate - startup_time))) "tps"
from v$sysstat s, v$instance i
where s.name in ('user commits','transaction rollbacks')
group by instance_name;
```
